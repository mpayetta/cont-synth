Generate a structured interview guide in clean markdown. Use the following format exactly — bold section headers, bullet-pointed questions, no numbered lists, no inline annotations.

**Opening**
Write one warm, open-ended question that invites the participant to walk you through a specific recent experience relevant to your product space. Use the "start with the recent past" technique — ask what they actually did, not what they think or prefer. This sets a behavioral tone for the whole conversation.

**Key Questions**
For each opportunity or theme above, write 2–3 probing questions. Rules:
- Group questions under a clearly labeled sub-section (e.g., "### Exploring: [short label]")
- Use behavioral framing ("Walk me through the last time...", "Tell me about a specific moment when...")
- Never ask hypotheticals ("Would you...", "Do you think...")
- Probe for frequency, consequences, workarounds, and emotional weight
- Target the specific opportunities listed above (or the additional direction if provided)

**Depth Probes**
Write 3–4 short follow-up probes the interviewer keeps in their back pocket to go deeper when a question lands. These should be short and open-ended, never leading (e.g., "What happened next?", "How often does that happen?", "What did you do instead?", "Who else was involved?").

**Closing**
Write 1–2 closing questions that invite the participant to surface anything important that wasn't covered, and close warmly.
