Now generate the guide. Follow Torres' interview principles throughout: start with the recent past, ask about specific experiences (not opinions), and never ask "would you" or "do you think" questions.

Rules for each question:
- Do NOT mention the opportunity statement verbatim or reveal any planned solution
- Do NOT ask leading questions ("Don't you think...") or hypotheticals ("Would you want...")
- DO use "Walk me through the last time you..." or "Tell me about a recent time when..." framing
- DO probe for frequency, workarounds, consequences, and emotional weight
- DO ask what they did next, not what they wish they could do
- DO craft questions that will reveal whether the planned solution approach matches how they actually work

For Assumption Probes: write 1–2 indirect behavioral questions per assumption to test whether it is grounded in reality. Never ask "Would you pay for X?" or reveal what you are testing.

If no assumptions were provided, skip the Assumption Probes section entirely.
